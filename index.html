About Government City College

A heritage institution with a strong academic tradition

Government City College, Hyderabad

Government City College (Autonomous), Nayapul, Hyderabad is a government higher education institution with a long academic heritage.

The college provides undergraduate education and opportunities for students to develop academic knowledge, technical skills and professional abilities.

The institution provides quality education in a wide range of disciplines and encourages students to participate in academic and co-curricular activities.

Located at Nayapul in Hyderabad, the college has an important place in the educational landscape of the city.

Computer Science And Applications

B.Sc Computer Science is an undergraduate program that provides a strong foundation in computer science principles, programming, and problem-solving techniques.

This program covers a wide range of topics including programming languages, data structures, databases, operating systems, computer networks, software engineering, web technologies and more.

This student portal provides a simple platform for organizing semester-wise notes and learning materials.

Students gain practical knowledge and hands-on experience to build a successful career in the field of computer science and IT industry.
